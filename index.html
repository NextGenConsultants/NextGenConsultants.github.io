Delivery approach

Six steps from business challenge to supported solution

You stay involved throughout — no long silences followed by a surprise demo.

1

Discover

Understand the business problem, current process, constraints and success measures.

Output
Problem statement and scope outline
Your involvement
Workshop with process owners
2

Assess

Review existing systems, data, licensing position and integration points.

Output
Assessment findings and options
Your involvement
Access to relevant stakeholders
3

Architect

Design the solution, data model, security model, environments and integration approach.

Output
Solution architecture and delivery plan
Your involvement
Design review and sign-off
4

Build

Iterative development with regular demonstrations and early feedback.

Output
Working increments and documentation
Your involvement
Sprint reviews and feedback
5

Validate & Deploy

6

Output

Your involvement
