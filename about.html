About NextGen Consultants

A boutique Microsoft technology consultancy founded by two experienced professionals, working with organisations and individuals across India and internationally.

Our story

Why we started

Between us we have spent years building solutions on the Microsoft stack — and watching a pattern repeat. Projects get delivered, they work on the day, and then they quietly become unmaintainable. Nobody documented the data model. Nobody knew which environment was authoritative. The person who built it moved on.

We started NextGen Consultants to work the other way round: design first, explain the reasoning, hand over properly, and make sure the client's own team can carry it forward. That is also why training and mentoring sit alongside consulting rather than being a separate business — they solve the same problem from the other end.

Our commitments

What you can hold us to

On responsible AI. We do not claim guaranteed accuracy from AI systems. AI suitability, model availability, licensing, data residency, and compliance requirements must be assessed for each organization and use case. Where the cost of an error is high, we design human review into the process rather than trusting the model.
On honest claims. This website contains no client logos, testimonials, case studies, project statistics or partner badges. That is deliberate. When we have genuine references to show, we will publish them with permission — and not before.
On ethical boundaries. Our mentoring and job support services have clear limits, published in full on the Mentoring & Job Support page. We will not impersonate candidates, misrepresent experience, or access systems without authorisation.
