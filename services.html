Governance maturity

A simple way to locate where you are today and what the next step looks like.

1

Initial

Apps appear organically. Nobody has a full inventory, and ownership is unclear.

2

Controlled

Basic DLP policies and environment separation exist. Creation is restricted.

3

Standardized

Naming, solution structure and ALM practices are documented and followed.

4

Managed

Inventory, monitoring and support models are in place. Ownership is tracked.

5

Optimized

Adoption is measured, makers are enabled, and the platform improves deliberately.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you work with small organisations, or only enterprises?
Both. Smaller organisations often benefit most from low-code, because a well-designed Power Platform solution can replace a system that would otherwise be unaffordable to build.
Can you work with our existing in-house team?
Yes. A common pattern is that we handle architecture and the harder technical pieces while your team builds alongside us and takes over afterwards. Knowledge transfer is part of every engagement.
We already have apps that have grown out of control. Can you help?
Yes. A technical audit establishes what exists, who owns it, what it depends on and where the risks are. That usually comes before any governance or consolidation work.
Do you provide ongoing support after go-live?
Yes, through a monthly retainer or a managed support arrangement. We also hand over documentation so you are not dependent on us for routine changes.
Which regions do you serve?
We work remotely with clients in India and internationally. Specific service regions: to be confirmed.

Not sure which service you need?

That is a normal place to start. A short discovery call is usually enough to work out the shape of the problem.
