Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need prior experience?
It depends on the course. Fundamentals courses assume no prior platform experience. Architecture and extensibility courses assume you have built something already. We confirm prerequisites before you enrol.
Are sessions live or pre-recorded?
Live and instructor-led. You can ask questions as they come up, which is most of the value.
Do you provide a certificate?
Certificates of completion are to be confirmed. Note that a completion certificate from us is not a Microsoft certification — we can help you prepare for those separately.
Can the syllabus be customised?
Yes, particularly for corporate batches. We start with a training needs assessment and build the module plan around it.
What happens after the course?
Many people continue with mentoring or job support while applying the material at work. That is optional and arranged separately.

Tell us what you want to learn

Send your current experience level and your goal, and we will recommend a track and a realistic timeline.
