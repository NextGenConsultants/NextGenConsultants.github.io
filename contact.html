Before you write

Frequently asked

What should I include in my enquiry?
The business problem, roughly what you have tried or already have in place, and any deadline. Technical detail is welcome but not required — we can work that out on the call.
Is the discovery call really free?
Yes. It is how we both work out whether the engagement makes sense. If it does not, we will say so.
Do you sign NDAs?
Yes, where an engagement requires it. Please do not send confidential material with an initial enquiry, though — a general description is enough at that stage.
Can you work in our time zone?
We work with clients in India and internationally and schedule around your availability. Tell us your time zone and preferred times.
