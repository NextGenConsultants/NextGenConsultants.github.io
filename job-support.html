Our ethical policy

NextGen Consultants provides mentoring, technical guidance, and learning support. We do not impersonate candidates, attend interviews on their behalf, misrepresent experience, complete unauthorized employment assessments, or access customer systems without proper authorization.

We state this first because the term "job support" is used loosely in this industry. What we offer is mentoring that makes you better at your job — not someone doing your job for you. If that is what you are looking for, we are not the right fit, and we would rather say so plainly than waste your time.

What we do

Types of support

Every session aims at you understanding the problem, not just clearing it.

Technical mentoring

Regular sessions working through whatever you are building, with the reasoning explained rather than just the answer.

Troubleshooting guidance

Working through a specific failure — how to isolate it, what the error is actually telling you, and how to avoid it next time.

Architecture guidance

A second opinion on a design before you commit to it, including the trade-offs you may not have considered.

Code & solution review

A structured review of what you have built, with specific, actionable feedback and an explanation of why each point matters.

Debugging assistance

Pairing on a live problem. You keep the keyboard — we work through it together so the method transfers.

Knowledge transfer

Structured handover of a topic or codebase so you can maintain and extend it independently.

Interview preparation

Practising how to explain your own work and reason about architecture out loud. Your experience, articulated better.

Skill-gap assessment

An honest read on where you are against where you want to be, and what to work on first.

Best-practice guidance

The patterns that hold up in production, and the common shortcuts that cause problems later.

How sessions run

Confidentiality

Confidentiality and data handling

Please do not share: production credentials, tenant administrator access, customer personal data, proprietary source code you are not authorised to share, or identification documents. We do not need any of it to help you, and asking you for it would put you in breach of your own employer's policies.

What we do

  • Work from sanitised or example data
  • Keep the content of sessions private
  • Discuss patterns, not your employer's specifics

What we will not do

  • Access your employer's or client's systems
  • Complete work that is submitted as your own unaided output
  • Represent you to any third party
Questions

Frequently asked

Will you attend my interview or assessment for me?
No. We prepare you so you can handle it yourself. Impersonating a candidate or completing an unauthorised assessment is explicitly outside what we do — see our ethical policy above.
Can you log into my company's environment to fix something?
No. We guide you while you work in your own environment. That keeps you compliant with your employer's access policies, and it means the knowledge stays with you.
Is this the same as training?
No. Training follows a syllabus and starts from fundamentals. Mentoring starts from whatever you are facing right now. Many people do training first, then mentoring while applying it.
How quickly can I get a session?
It depends on current availability and your time zone. Send your requirement and we will confirm realistic options.
What if my problem turns out to be beyond your expertise?
We tell you, and we do not charge you for finding that out. Pretending otherwise would waste your time and money.

Describe what you are stuck on

A short description is enough for us to say whether we can help and how long it is likely to take.
